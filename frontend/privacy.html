Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

1. Introduction & Core Privacy Commitment

At Finora (Financial Habit Builder & Wealth Growth Tracker), we hold a fundamental principle: your financial data belongs solely to you. We built this platform so you can track habits, expenses, and asset growth with absolute peace of mind.

We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information or financial records to third parties or advertising networks.

2. Data We Collect & How We Use It

We collect only the minimum necessary information to provide secure account access and render your financial analytics:

  • Account Identity: Your display name, email address, salted password hash, and preferred currency token.
  • Financial Entries: Income records, categorized expense logs, habit completion streaks, savings goal targets, and investment asset entries manually submitted by you.
  • Technical Session Logs: Standard HTTP request logs (IP address, user agent, timestamps) for performance and anti-fraud monitoring.

3. Storage, Encryption & Security Standards

All account data is stored in isolated, encrypted database schemas (SQLite / Turso DB) with industry-standard TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 at rest. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with high cost factors. Session authentication is enforced via signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT).

Finora does not require or store sensitive bank account credentials, credit card numbers, or SSN/tax identification numbers.

4. Analytics & Essential Cookies

We use strictly essential cookies and local storage tokens (fhb_token, wealthpulse_theme, fhb_cookie_consent) to maintain your login session and interface theme settings. We do not employ third-party tracking pixels or behavioral cross-site trackers.

5. GDPR Compliance & Your Rights

Under GDPR and international privacy standards, you hold full control over your data:

  • Right of Access & Export: You can download a complete JSON export of all your financial records at any time using the Data Export feature in your account settings.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You can permanently delete your account and all associated records using the account deletion workflow, which immediately purges all database rows.
  • Right to Rectification: You may edit or correct any income, expense, or goal entry in real time through the dashboard interface.

6. Data Retention Policy

Your financial data is retained for as long as your account remains active. If an account is inactive for more than 24 consecutive months or upon explicit deletion request, all records are permanently deleted from active storage and automated backups.

7. Contact Information

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your data privacy rights, please contact our Data Protection Team at privacy@finora.app.
